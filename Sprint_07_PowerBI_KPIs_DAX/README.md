# Sprint 07 - Power BI KPIs and DAX

This project is part of my Data Analytics bootcamp portfolio at IT Academy / Barcelona Activa.

The main goal of this sprint was to create an interactive Power BI report focused on business KPIs, sales performance, customer behavior and data-driven decision-making.

The report is based on a fictional retail/business case called Puidgemarket and includes several analytical pages with KPIs, charts, tables, filters and business insights.

---

## Main Objectives

- Build a Power BI report from business data
- Create KPI cards for key business indicators
- Analyze annual sales performance
- Compare results against business targets
- Identify seasonal sales patterns
- Analyze store and country performance
- Explore customer behavior
- Segment customers by age range
- Compare average transaction values
- Analyze accepted and rejected transactions
- Use visual storytelling to communicate insights clearly

## Report Structure

The Power BI report includes several pages focused on different business questions.

---

### Page 1 - Annual KPI Review and Sales Overview

This page provides a general overview of business performance.

It includes:

- Annual sales evolution from 2015 to 2024
- Annual revenue compared with a target of 2.6 million dollars
- Monthly sales seasonality over the last 5 years
- Store performance by country
- Average sales per company
- Loyalty program table based on customer average purchase
- Average transaction comparison between 2021 and 2022

Key elements include KPI cards, line charts, seasonal column charts and business tables.

---

### Page 2 - Germany Market Analysis 2018–2019

This page focuses on the German market between 2018 and 2019.

It includes:

- Revenue in 2018 and 2019
- Number of stores in Germany
- German customer activity
- Monthly sales trends for 2018 and 2019
- Comparison between Germany and other countries
- Average sales per company
- Average transaction value
- Top products sold in Germany
- Top customer by order volume

This page demonstrates how Power BI can be used to analyze a specific market and compare its performance over time.

---

### Page 3 - Customer Analysis

This page focuses on customer-level behavior.

It includes a detailed table with:

- Customer name
- Number of purchases
- Total spending
- Average purchase value
- Median purchase value
- Most expensive product purchased
- Cheapest product purchased

It also includes product analysis by age range, showing the most and least purchased products for each age group.

---

### Page 4 - Customer Behavior Dashboard

This page presents an interactive dashboard focused on customer behavior.

It includes:

- Total number of users
- Most purchased product
- Least purchased product
- Units sold
- Average transaction value
- Age range slicer
- Customer transactions by country
- Customer distribution by country
- Purchase volume by age range
- Average transaction by age range
- Customers above and below the average purchase threshold
- Acceptance rate of transactions

This page combines KPI cards, slicers, treemap, donut chart and bar charts to create an executive-style dashboard.

---

## Key Insights

Some of the main insights shown in the report include:

- Annual sales are compared against a target of 2.6 million dollars.
- Monthly sales patterns help identify seasonality and commercial optimization opportunities.
- Germany, France, Spain, United Kingdom and Italy show relevant sales performance indicators.
- In the Germany analysis, the report compares 2018 and 2019 performance and highlights key products and customers.
- Customer behavior can be segmented by age range and country.
- The acceptance rate of transactions is very high, with rejected transactions representing a small percentage.
- Product performance varies across customer age segments.
