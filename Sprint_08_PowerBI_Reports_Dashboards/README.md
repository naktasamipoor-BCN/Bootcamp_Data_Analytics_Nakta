# Sprint 08 - Power BI Reports and Dashboards

This project is part of my Data Analytics bootcamp portfolio at IT Academy / Barcelona Activa.

The main goal of this sprint was to design a Power BI report focused on business storytelling, sales performance analysis and dashboard presentation.

The report is based on a fictional business case called Puidgemarket and is prepared as a presentation for an annual meeting with data analysts in Barcelona.

This sprint focuses not only on creating visualizations, but also on building a clear analytical narrative: understanding what happened, identifying where the signals are, diagnosing the sales drop and presenting insights in a structured way.

---

## Main Objectives

- Create a multi-page Power BI report
- Analyze sales performance over the last five years
- Identify revenue trends and business changes
- Compare annual income and transaction volume
- Detect seasonal sales patterns
- Analyze country-level sales performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Diagnose the sales drop in 2023
- Compare transaction volume and average ticket
- Explore market concentration and company performance
- Use slicers and filters for interactive analysis

## Report Structure

The report is organized into several pages, each focused on a specific analytical question.

---

### Page 1 - What Happened in the Last 5 Years?

This page provides an executive overview of the business performance over the last five years.

It analyzes:

- Annual revenue evolution
- Spending per user
- Total transaction volume
- Sales by country
- Average sales by company and country
- Product storage structure

Main findings include:

- Revenue increased until reaching its peak in 2021.
- From 2022 onward, revenue started to decline.
- In 2023, revenue reached one of the lowest points in the period.
- 2024 shows a partial recovery but does not fully recover the 2021–2022 level.
- Transaction volume changed less than revenue, suggesting that the problem may not be only related to order count.
- Sweden and the Netherlands lead total sales, but Germany appears as a high-value market when analyzing average sales per company.

---

### Page 2 - Patterns and Segments

This page focuses on identifying signals behind the overall business trend.

It analyzes:

- Monthly sales seasonality
- Product sales ranking
- Revenue and transaction trend together
- Top winners and losers by country
- Regional and country filters

Main findings include:

- March, September and December are the strongest sales months.
- February, August and November are weaker months.
- Seasonal patterns are stable year after year.
- Transaction volume follows a similar pattern to revenue, with a peak around 2021 and a drop in 2023.
- Germany concentrates a large part of the sales decline from 2022 to 2023.
- This suggests that the decline is not necessarily a global business problem, but may be concentrated in specific markets.

---

### Page 3 - Diagnosis: Why Did Sales Fall in 2023?

This page investigates the possible reasons behind the 2023 sales decline.

It analyzes:

- Transaction volume vs average ticket
- Product sales comparison between 2022 and 2023
- Performance of the top companies
- The specific case of Ac Fermentum Incorporated

Main findings include:

- The decline may be more related to a reduction in transaction volume than to a strong drop in average ticket.
- Some key companies may have a significant impact on the business decline.
- Ac Fermentum Incorporated appears as a relevant case because it moved from being one of the leading companies to no longer appearing in the top 3 after 2023.
- This suggests a possible concentration risk in the business model.

---

### Page 4 - Interactive Exploration

This page includes interactive filters and exploratory visuals.

It allows analysis by:

- Country
- Region
- Age range
- Sales amount
- Rejected transaction rate
- Monthly seasonality
- Market performance

The page includes slicers and filter buttons that make the report more interactive and suitable for presentation or business exploration.

---

### Page 5 - Additional Business Exploration

This page continues the diagnostic analysis with additional visuals.

It includes:

- Product sales comparison between 2022 and 2023
- The case of Ac Fermentum Incorporated over time
- Analysis of small markets with high average value
- Scatter plot comparing transaction volume, average ticket and sales by country

This page supports deeper exploration of business opportunities and risks.

---

## Key Insights

Some of the main insights from the report are:

- Revenue peaked in 2021 and declined afterward.
- 2023 was the most concerning year in the analyzed period.
- 2024 shows partial recovery, but not enough to return to the previous peak.
- The number of transactions changed less dramatically than revenue.
- Germany is one of the markets with the strongest negative impact after 2022.
- Monthly seasonality is stable, with March, September and December as stronger months.
- Some companies have a disproportionate impact on total sales performance.
- Ac Fermentum Incorporated is a key case that may explain part of the sales decline.
- Rejected transactions show an unusual peak in 2024 and should be investigated further.
- There may be smaller markets with high average value that deserve strategic attention.

This sprint helped me understand how to move from simple visualizations to a complete business report.

I practiced how to structure an analytical story, organize report pages, use visuals to answer business questions and guide the viewer from general overview to diagnosis and possible action areas.
